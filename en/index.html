In development · coming soon

A smart running coach,straight to your watch.

A personal running plan built around your level and goal. Each planned run is sent to your watch as a structured workout, and the run you complete comes back to 2Act so the plan can adapt to you. We are building connections to Garmin and Apple Watch.

  1. 1. A plan built for you

    Easy runs, intervals, tempo and long runs, based on your level, the days you have and your goal.

  2. 2. The workout waits on your watch

    Every planned run is published to your Garmin Connect calendar as a structured workout: warm-up, intervals with pace or heart-rate targets, and cool-down. Your watch guides you step by step.

  3. 3. The plan learns from you

    When you finish and sync, the run summary (distance, duration, pace, heart rate) comes back to 2Act. The session is marked done and your next sessions are adjusted by our own server-side training rules.

Syncing

Your watch data stays yours

  • Stored only in our own database (Supabase), encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Never sold, never used for advertising and never sent to analytics tools.
  • Never sent to any AI or machine-learning service and never used to train models, including Gia.
  • Disconnect anytime from the app settings: syncing stops and connection tokens are deleted.

Full details in our Privacy Policy, section 3a: Garmin watch connection.

Why connect a watch?

Running already lives in 2Act: GPS tracking, intervals, treadmill mode and finish screens. The next step is leaving the phone at home and getting the workout on your wrist. A watch connection closes the loop: the workout comes from the plan, is done on the watch, and comes back to shape the next one.
